Shimokitazawa下北沢

Open now

GETTING THEREAbout 7 minutes from Shinjuku on the Odakyu Line, or 3 minutes from Shibuya on the Keio Inokashira Line.

THE AREATokyo's thrift capital. Shops ring the station in every direction — the big chains on the south and east streets, indie vintage on the north side (Ichibangai). Everything is within a 10-minute stroll, and most shops open around 11 AM to noon, so arrive at lunchtime and just wander.

Koenji高円寺

Open now

GETTING THEREAbout 6 minutes from Shinjuku on the JR Chuo Line. Rapid trains stop at Koenji on weekends; local Sobu Line trains stop all day.

THE AREAFrom the south exit, the covered PAL arcade runs straight ahead and turns into the open-air Look Shotengai — nearly every shop on this list lines that one street or sits just off it. Serious American vintage at SAFARI, brand resale at the three TreFacStyle stores, tiny specialists in between.

Harajuku & Shibuya原宿・渋谷

Open now

GETTING THEREHarajuku Station (Takeshita Exit) or Meiji-jingumae Station — the shops split between Cat Street, Takeshita-dori and the Omotesando side streets.

THE AREAThe priciest vintage in Tokyo: museum-grade American denim at BerBerJin, huge imported floors at JAM and Chicago, designer resale at Kindal and KINJI. All five shops sit within a 10-minute walk of each other.

Open now

Open now

THE AREAThe market street under the tracks is the walk itself: military surplus at Nakata Shoten, big recycle floors at Mode Off and MAGAZINES, bargain racks at Tanpopo House. Loud, dense and fun.

Good to know

  • Many Tokyo thrift shops open at 11 AM or noon, and small ones close early — check the live badge before a long trip.
  • Shimokitazawa and Koenji are dense enough to cover on foot in one afternoon each. Start with the shops marked OPEN NOW.
  • Most shops take cards, but small vintage stores may be cash only. Tax-free shopping is rare in thrift stores.
  • Hours were checked on Nov 24, 2026 — a quick look at the Google listing before you go is wise.
