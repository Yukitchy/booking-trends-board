GUEST GUIDEIKEBUKURO · TOKYO

An evening in Ikebukuro

Anime · Games · Sunshine CityFun for the whole family
Evening basics — the short version

For

Families and anime fans — especially anyone who has done Akihabara and wants the other otaku capital.

Why

The flagships in one place: Japan's largest Pokémon Center, the Animate flagship and the world's biggest gashapon hall, minutes apart.

Where

East side of Ikebukuro Station — most stops inside Sunshine City. No trains in between, no long walks.

Good to know

Shops open until 20:00–21:00 · tax-free counters — bring passports · no alcohol anywhere, so younger guests are fine.

Anime & games

6 STOPS

Ikebukuro's side of otaku Tokyo — bigger flagships, less maze. Pick any of them, in any order.

Pokémon Center Mega Tokyo

10:00 – 20:00

Sunshine City Alpa 2F · ポケモンセンターメガトウキョー

Japan's largest Pokémon Center — limited Mega Tokyo goods, a Charizard statue for photos and the Pikachu Sweets takeout counter. The Mugiwara Store, the official One Piece shop, sits on the same floor.

Mugiwara Store

10:00 – 20:00

Sunshine City Alpa 2F · 麦わらストア 池袋店

The official One Piece store, a few steps from the Pokémon Center (the photo shows the Sunshine City atrium). Store-only goods and a Thousand Sunny photo spot — an easy win for One Piece fans.

Gashapon Department Store

10:00 – 21:00

Sunshine City, World Import Mart 3F · ガシャポンデパートメント池袋総本店

Over 3,000 capsule-toy machines in one hall — billed as the largest gashapon store in the world. The Bandai Namco Cross Store on the same floor covers the One Piece Card Game and Tamashii Nations figures.

Animate Ikebukuro

10:00 – 21:00

2 min from Sunshine City · アニメイト池袋本店

Nine floors — one of the biggest anime shops in Japan, and Animate's flagship. New manga, figures, CDs and limited goods, plus event spaces upstairs: the department-store version of Akihabara.

JUMP SHOP

11:00 – 21:00

Ikebukuro PARCO B2F, at the East Exit · ジャンプショップ 池袋店

The official Weekly Shonen Jump store: One Piece next to Naruto, Dragon Ball and Jujutsu Kaisen, with items sold nowhere else. A compact last stop on the way back to the trains. (Photo shows another branch.)

BOOKOFF Sunshine 60 Street

10:00 – 22:00

Sunshine 60 Street · ブックオフ サンシャイン60通り店

Two floors of second-hand manga, figures, anime goods and Pokémon cards. The treasure-hunt stop: out-of-print volumes and bargain figures that never reach the flagship stores.

Sunshine City, after dark

3 STOPS

The same complex also holds a rooftop aquarium, a planetarium and a 60th-floor observatory — the calm half of the evening.

Sunshine 60 Observatory TENBOU-PARK

11:00 – 21:00 · last entry 20:30

Sunshine 60, 60F · サンシャイン60展望台 てんぼうパーク

The top of Sunshine 60, 251 metres up, rebuilt as an indoor park — lawn, benches and big sky. Sunset over the city, then the night view, without leaving the building.

Sunshine Aquarium

10:00 – 18:00 in winter

World Import Mart rooftop · サンシャイン水族館

A rooftop aquarium famous for the Sky Penguin tank, where penguins appear to fly over the city, and sea lions swimming in a ring tank above your head. It closes earlier than the shops, so it suits the late-afternoon end of the plan.

Planetarium MANTEN

Shows 11:00 – 20:00

World Import Mart rooftop · コニカミノルタプラネタリウム満天

Konica Minolta's dome theatre: reclining seats, a star field over the whole ceiling and programmes with music. A calm, sit-down hour that rests everyone's feet before dinner.
