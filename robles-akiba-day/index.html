Daniela's Birthday in Akihabara

Sun 6 Dec · 11:00 to 15:00 · A private tour for Victor, Ines & Daniela
FOR
Victor, Ines & Daniela — a birthday trip built around Daniela's favourites: Evangelion, Ergo Proxy and Serial Experiments Lain.
WHEN
Sunday 6 December, 11:00 to 15:00. Four hours, at your pace — we can linger where Daniela loves it most.
MEET
Inside Vie de France, BiTO AKIBA (1F), right by Akihabara Station. Open in Google Maps — please use this link, there is another Vie de France on the other side of the station.
FIRST
Radio Kaikan, for Evangelion figures and goods while the day is fresh.
THEN
The vintage hunt: Mandarake and Book Off, where Lain and Ergo Proxy treasures from the 90s and 2000s still turn up.

Places to visit

📍
FIGURES & GOODS

01 · Radio Kaikan

10:00–20:00

Ten floors of small shops: figures, cards and character goods. The Evangelion shelves here are some of the best in Akihabara — we pick the floors that match Daniela's favourites.

📍
VINTAGE & RARE

02 · Mandarake Complex

12:00–20:00

Eight floors of second-hand treasure. This is where out-of-print Lain and Ergo Proxy DVDs, art books and goods still surface — the deep-cut stop of the day.

📍
PRIZE FIGURES

03 · Trader

Big and bright, with many prize figures at good prices — a kind place to find an Evangelion figure without the collector markup.

📍
SECOND-HAND MEDIA

04 · Book Off Akihabara Ekimae

10:00–22:00

Second-hand manga, CDs, DVDs and games right by the station. Old anime DVDs — Lain included — often hide here at pocket-money prices.

📍
NEW GOODS

05 · Animate Akihabara

10:00–21:00

Several floors of new anime and manga goods from many series, plus a floor of capsule toys in the Annex building.

📍
APPAREL

06 · Cospa G-Store Akiba

10:00–20:00

Character T-shirts, apparel and goods — NERV-style designs included — on the 4th floor of the Onoden main building.

📍
CAPSULE TOYS

07 · Akihabara Gachapon Kaikan

Opens 11:00

Hundreds of capsule toy machines in one shop. A few birthday tries — keep whatever comes out.

📍
ARCADE

08 · GiGO Akihabara

from 10:00

Crane games and prizes — a good place to play for a while if the group wants a break from shopping.
