Japanese for Anime & Manga Fans

Your N5 Study Plan

Started hiragana on Duolingo? That's your tutorial stage cleared. Here is the full quest line to JLPT N5.

GOAL: JLPT N5TIME: 4–6 MONTHS*PARTY: 1 LESSON / WEEK
Lv.0
Stage 0 — First Village

Hiragana & Katakana

Learn to read and write both kana alphabets. No more romaji — real Japanese from the start, with words from anime you know.

About 2–3 weeks
Lv.1
Stage 1 — Skill Tree

Core Words & Basic Grammar

Unlock the most common words and sentence patterns: です・ます forms, particles は・が・を・に・へ, questions and past tense.

About 6–8 weeks
Lv.2
Stage 2 — Free Roam

Talk About Anime & Manga

Take your skills into the open world: talk about your favorite series, characters and scenes, out loud, every lesson.

About 6–8 weeks
Lv.3
Final Stage — Boss Prep

N5 Practice

Train in the real JLPT N5 format — vocabulary, grammar, reading and listening — and patch up your weak spots.

About 4–6 weeks
How a week looksOnline on italki · 60 minutes per lesson · light homework (10–15 min reviews). *4–6 months is a rough guide at one lesson a week — everyone's pace is different.
