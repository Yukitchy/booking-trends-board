YOUR TEACHER

The senpai who actually watched them

I'm Yuuki, a native Japanese teacher based in Tokyo. I studied abroad myself, so I remember what it feels like to understand every word in a sentence and still not be able to say anything back. That's the gap my lessons are built around.

I've watched over 1,500 anime titles — not as a marketing line, but because it's what I do. So when you quote a line and ask "does anyone really say that?", you'll get a straight answer, and usually a better way to say it.

Outside of lessons I run a Japanese podcast, a Discord community called NihonGO School, and guided tours of Akihabara. Different rooms, same job: getting people to use Japanese out loud.

Questions before you book

What if I have never taken a lesson before?

Start with the 30-minute trial lesson ($13). We introduce ourselves and decide together how your lessons should work. Everyone starts here.

Do I need to set my timezone?

No. The booking page detects your timezone from your browser and shows every slot in your local time.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 12 hours before the lesson. Within 12 hours, or if you do not show up within 15 minutes of the start time, the lesson counts as taken.

How do the lessons happen?

On Google Meet. Pay by card when you book, and the link is in your confirmation email right away.

Is it really about anime, or just any native speaker?

Your tutor is a Tokyo native who works anime, manga and music into lessons on request. Mention your favorite shows in the trial lesson and I'll bring in real examples from day one.
